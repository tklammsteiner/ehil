# EHIL

**Einsatz von Holzkohle in der industriellen Landwirtschaft** (Use of charcoal in industrial agriculture)

EHIL is a practice-oriented research project at MCI Innsbruck. It tests whether adding charcoal to compost can help protect climate, water and soil in agriculture. This repository holds the project website, which presents the study background, data and microbial community results.

**Website:** https://tklammsteiner.github.io/ehil/index.html

## Background

Austrian farming faces drought, heavy rain, pests and harvest losses. EHIL looks at charcoal-enriched compost as a response. The charcoal is a byproduct of a local wood-fired combined heat and power plant. The project aims to:

- produce high-quality compost from regional biogenic resources
- keep nutrients from washing out of farmland
- store water in the soil, especially in dry periods
- store carbon long-term
- create regional value

Early results suggest charcoal can speed up composting. Benefits such as water retention and nutrient retention are expected effects that still have to be shown in the field.

## Methods

Field trials test the compost. Soil and compost microbial communities were profiled by amplicon sequencing on an Illumina MiSeq:

| Target | Marker region | Primers |
|--------|---------------|---------|
| Bacteria | 16S rRNA gene, V3-4 | 341F / 805R |
| Fungi | ITS2 | ITS3 / ITS4 |
